# Vision

GeoBeacon is a public situational-awareness and action platform for understanding environmental crises and coordinating response.

It turns verified satellite, emergency, and public data into understandable incidents on a live map. People and response organizations should be able to see what is happening, assess its spatial context, and act on shared, timely information.

GeoBeacon prioritizes trustworthy incident data, clear provenance, real-time updates, and reliable geospatial analysis. The product should evolve from displaying events to supporting coordinated response.

## Long-term outcomes

- Public users can quickly understand the location, severity, and development of environmental incidents.
- Responders can coordinate around a shared, current operational picture.
- Spatial analysis is reliable enough to support decisions, not merely visualization.

## Success horizon

Within 2–3 years, success means enabling coordinated real-time response at meaningful scale, with reliable geospatial analysis as the foundation for trustworthy decisions.
